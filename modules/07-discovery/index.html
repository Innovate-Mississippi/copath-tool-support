The "Mom Test" Rules

  • Rule 1: Talk about their life, not your idea.
  • Rule 2: Ask about specifics in the past, not generics or opinions about the future.
  • Rule 3: Talk less, listen more.

Consulting FAQ: Next Moves

What if they complain, but haven't tried to fix it?

Then it's not a real problem. If a problem is painful enough to pay for, they will have already tried to hack together a solution (even if it's just a spreadsheet). Move on.

What if they say "I love this, let me know when it's done"?

That is a Polite Lie. Force a commitment. Reply: "We are building a pilot group next month. It requires a $500 deposit to secure your spot. Should I send you the link?" Their answer to that is your actual data.
