The "Unit Economics Auditor." Stress-test your price against industry venture floors and CAC reality.

1. Industry Context

Each industry has a venture-backable ACV floor. Below it, you're a lifestyle business.

2. Your Numbers

Consulting Guidance

The 3:1 Rule. A venture-grade SaaS business needs LTV at least 3x CAC. Below that, you're spending more to acquire customers than you'll ever recoup.

The 33% CAC Ceiling. If CAC eats more than a third of your first year's revenue, your payback period is too long for VC math to work.

Industry Floors. Different sectors have different floors. A $5k ACV is fine for retail e-commerce but a death sentence for biotech (where the floor is $100k+).
